# Figmastic


Project Name:VoltRide

Project Idea:
A smart mobility platform that relies on electric bicycles to reduce congestion and pollution in cities, providing a safe, affordable, and environmentally friendly means of transportation.

The platform includes a passenger app that offers quick booking and live tracking of trips, as well as a discounted subscription system and ride-sharing options. It also provides an app for bike owners to manage bikes and fares, and obtain accurate statistics on profits and trips, with city dashboards that provide live data and environmental and economic reports.

The platform boasts innovative features such as a rewards system, group rides, smart charging stations, and the integration of artificial intelligence to predict demand and improve the user experience.
